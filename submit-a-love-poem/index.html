Before you send

  • Free, always. No contest, no fee, no prize.
  • Any language and any kind of love: romantic, family, friendship, grief.
  • Family-friendly only: no profanity, explicit content or hate.
  • One poem at a time, please.
  • We publish it as you wrote it, spelling and all, unless you ask us to fix something.
  • Want it taken down later? Just ask. No reason needed.
