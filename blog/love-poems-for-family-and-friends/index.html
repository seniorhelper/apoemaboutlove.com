Love poems for family and friends

By the A Poem About Love editors · Updated November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Write a love poem freeGet published
Quick answer

Love poems for family and friends work the same way romantic ones do: one specific memory, one detail only you would know, and a plain statement of what they mean to you. The ancient Greeks even had separate words for these loves: storge for family, philia for friendship and agape for selfless love.

When people search for “a poem about love,” a lot of them aren't writing to a partner. They're writing to a mom on her birthday, a brother who moved across the country, a best friend getting married or a grandparent who's gone. These are love poems too, and some of the deepest ones.

Four kinds of love, four kinds of poem

The ancient Greeks used different words for different loves. They're a handy way to think about what you're writing:

  • Eros is romantic, passionate love. Most love poems live here.
  • Storge is the steady love of family. Parents, children and siblings.
  • Philia is the love between true friends.
  • Agape is selfless, giving love, often used in a spiritual sense.

For a mother or father

Write about something they did over and over that you only appreciated later: the packed lunches, the rides, the late-night talks.

You drove me to every practice and waited in the car with the radio low. I thought you were bored. Now I know you were just glad to be the one who took me.

For a brother or sister

Siblings share a private language of old jokes, blame and loyalty. Use it. For a poem written in grief for a little brother, read “I Love You Brother” on The Notebook page.

For a best friend

You've seen every version of me, the bad haircut years, the broken heart, the new job, the old car, and somehow you kept all of my phone numbers.

For someone who's gone

An elegy is a love poem for someone who has died. Write to them, not about them, as if they could still read it. Use their name. Tell them one thing you never got to say. If grief is heavy right now, writing can help carry it, and so can talking to the people around you.

Yes. Love poems can be for anyone you love: a parent, child, sibling, grandparent or best friend. Many of the most moving love poems are about family.

A poem mourning someone who has died is called an elegy. It's one of the oldest forms of love poem.
