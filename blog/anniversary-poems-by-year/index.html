Anniversary poems by year

By the A Poem About Love editors · Updated November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Write a love poem freeGet published
Quick answer

Match your anniversary poem to the traditional gift for that year: paper for the 1st, cotton for the 2nd, leather for the 3rd, wood for the 5th, tin for the 10th, crystal for the 15th, china for the 20th, silver for the 25th, pearl for the 30th, ruby for the 40th and gold for the 50th. Write the poem around what that material means.

Traditional anniversary gifts give you a built-in theme. Paper is fragile and new, gold is rare and lasting, and each material says something about a marriage at that stage. Here's an idea and a short original poem for the milestone years.

1st anniversary: paper

New, blank and full of possibility. Write about the first page of your story.

One year of pages. Most of them coffee-stained, all of them ours.

2nd: cotton

Soft, everyday, the fabric of ordinary life.

Your old t-shirt, my favorite pajamas. That's what two years feels like: worn in, and warmer for it.

A Poem About Love

3rd: leather

Tough, protective, and better with age.

We've been scuffed a little. Good leather is supposed to be.

A Poem About Love

5th: wood

Roots and rings. Something that grows slowly and holds weight.

Five rings in the trunk of us. I'd count a hundred more.

A Poem About Love

10th: tin or aluminum

Durable and flexible: a love that bends without breaking.

Ten years of bending. Not once did we break.

A Poem About Love

15th: crystal

Clear and bright. Write about seeing each other clearly.

Fifteen years and I can see right through you, and I love every single thing I see.

A Poem About Love

20th: china

Fine, but meant to be used. Write about everyday dinners and full tables.

25th: silver

The big quarter-century. Silver hair, silver light, silver lining.

Your hair has gone silver at the temples. You keep apologizing. I keep thinking: look how lucky we got.

A Poem About Love

30th: pearl

Something beautiful made slowly out of grit.

40th: ruby

Deep red, the color of a love that's still burning.

50th: gold

Rare and lasting. Write about the whole road.

Fifty years at the same kitchen table. We never sanded out the scratches. Some things should show how much they've been loved.

Gift-list traditions vary a little between sources and countries, so feel free to pick the material that fits your story best. For longer anniversary poems, see the anniversary section of our love poems, or make one with the love poem generator using the “Anniversary” tone.

Questions

Common traditional themes include paper (1st), cotton (2nd), leather (3rd), wood (5th), tin (10th), crystal (15th), china (20th), silver (25th), pearl (30th), ruby (40th) and gold (50th). Lists vary slightly by source.

For a card, four to eight lines. For a speech at a party, twelve to twenty lines is about one minute read aloud.
