Short love poems for texts and cards

By the A Poem About Love editors · Updated November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Write a love poem freeGet published
Quick answer

The best short love poem for a text or card is two to four lines long, names one real detail about the person, and ends on a single strong image. Copy one below, or use the 3-line method to write your own.

A card has room for about four lines before your handwriting starts to shrink. A text has even less, because people read texts with one thumb, in line at the coffee shop. So a short love poem isn't a lesser poem. It's a poem built for where it will actually be read.

The 3-line method for writing your own

  1. Line 1: a real, small detail. “You still hum while you make coffee.” Specific beats grand every time.
  2. Line 2: what that detail does to you. “I pretend not to listen.”
  3. Line 3: the turn. Say the big thing in plain words. “I've never heard a better song.”

That's a complete short love poem. If you'd like help getting started, our free love poem generator builds one around a name and a detail, and you can edit it from there.

Tips for sending a love poem by text

  • Send it alone. Don't bury it under “also, did you pay the electric bill?”
  • Use line breaks. Hit return between lines. A poem that runs together reads like a paragraph.
  • Skip the explanation. “I wrote this lol” takes the air out of it. Let it stand.
  • Timing matters. Mid-afternoon on a hard workday lands harder than midnight.
