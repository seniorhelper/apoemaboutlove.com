How to read a love poem out loud

By the A Poem About Love editors · Updated November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Write a love poem freeGet published
Quick answer

Read a love poem out loud at about half your normal talking speed, pause at the end of every line, breathe at every stanza break, and look up at the person once. Practice five times out loud before the day. If you get emotional, stop, breathe and smile. Everyone will wait.

A poem on the page and a poem in the air are two different animals. Read aloud well, even a simple love poem can make a room go quiet. Here's how to do it.

Slow down, then slow down more

Nerves make everyone rush. Aim for roughly half your normal speaking speed. It will feel painfully slow to you and sound just right to everyone listening.

Pause at line breaks

Poets break lines on purpose. A tiny pause at the end of each line, even without a comma, lets the listener hear the shape of the poem. Take a full breath at every blank line between stanzas.

Mark up your copy

  • Print it in large type, 16 to 18 point, double-spaced.
  • Draw a slash where you'll breathe.
  • Underline the one or two words that matter most.
  • Number the pages if there's more than one.

Look up, but not too much

Look up at the person, or the couple, once or twice: at the start, and before the last line. The last line is the one to deliver looking right at them.

If you get emotional

It happens at weddings and funerals, and honestly that's part of the gift. Stop. Breathe in through your nose. Smile if you can. Then pick up from the start of the line. Nobody in the room minds waiting for you.

A five-day practice routine

  1. Day 1: read it silently three times.
  2. Day 2: read it aloud to yourself.
  3. Day 3: record yourself on your phone and listen.
  4. Day 4: read it to one trusted person.
  5. Day 5: read it once, standing up, as if it's the day.
