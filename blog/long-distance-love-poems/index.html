Long distance love poems

By the A Poem About Love editors · Updated November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Write a love poem freeGet published
Quick answer

A good long distance love poem focuses on a shared detail that crosses the distance, like the same moon, a sweatshirt that still smells like them, or a countdown, rather than only on how far apart you are. Name the miles, then name what connects you anyway.

Distance does a strange thing to love. It makes every small object louder: a voice note, a hoodie, a calendar. That's good news for poems, because small objects are what poems are made of.

Ideas for writing your own

  • The shared sky. The same moon, the same storm crossing both your states.
  • The object. Something of theirs you keep close.
  • The countdown. Days, flights, how you'll greet them at the airport.
  • The routine. The goodnight call, falling asleep on video, the photo of their coffee.
  • The reunion. Write the first five seconds of seeing them again.

Ways to send it that feel closer

Mail a handwritten copy. Paper that their hands will touch after yours shrinks the distance. Or record yourself reading it as a voice note. Hearing it in your voice is half the poem.
