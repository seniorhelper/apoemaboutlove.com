why they're left as written

Why we don't fix the spelling

A notebook poem is a record of a moment. The crossed-out word, the line squeezed in sideways, the spelling that came out at two in the morning: that is the poem as it was actually felt. Cleaning it up would make it neater and less true.

Where a word on the page was truly unreadable, it's been smoothed so the line still makes sense. Everything else is exactly as it sits on the paper. The rest of Zachary's poems are in the founding collection on the home page, and if you keep a notebook like this too, we'd love to read it.
