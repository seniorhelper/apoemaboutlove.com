The best poetry resources

Trusted places to read great love poems, learn the craft and find somewhere to publish your own.

Poetry Foundation

A huge free library of poems and poet biographies, plus Poetry magazine.

www.poetryfoundation.org ↗

Academy of American Poets (poets.org)

Poem-a-Day, thousands of poems, and lesson plans. Its love poem anthologies are a great place to start.

poets.org ↗

Library of Congress Poetry & Literature Center

Home of the U.S. Poet Laureate, with recordings of poets reading their own work.

www.loc.gov/programs/poetry-and-literature ↗

Poets & Writers

The best free database of literary magazines, contests and small presses for poets who want to publish.

www.pw.org ↗

Project Gutenberg

Free public-domain books, including the classic love poets: Shakespeare, Browning, Keats, Dickinson and more.

www.gutenberg.org ↗

U.S. Copyright Office

Official information on copyright and how to register your work if you choose to.

www.copyright.gov ↗
