Submission terms

The plain-English rules for sending us a poem: you keep your copyright, there's no payment, and publishing isn't guaranteed.

Last updated November 17, 2026

These terms apply when you send a poem, or anything else, to apoemaboutlove.com (“the site,” “we,” “us”). By submitting, you agree to them.

1. Your poem must be yours

You confirm the poem is your own original work, that you have the right to share it, and that it doesn't copy anyone else's writing, including song lyrics or other poems. If you translated someone else's poem, tell us who wrote it. We may not be able to publish it.

2. You keep your copyright

Submitting does not transfer ownership. You keep all copyright in your poem.

3. The license you give us

You give apoemaboutlove.com a free, non-exclusive, worldwide license to publish, display, format, archive and share your poem on the site and on the site's own social channels, credited to the name you provided. Non-exclusive means you can publish your poem anywhere else you like.

4. No payment

Submitting is free and no payment is made to anyone. No fees, prizes, royalties or compensation of any kind are owed, whether or not your poem is published, and even if the page it appears on displays ads.

5. No guarantee of publication

Every submission is read, but we don't publish everything. We decide, at our sole discretion, whether and when a poem is published, and we may decline without giving a reason.

6. Family-friendly content only

We publish poems suitable for all ages. Poems containing profanity, sexually explicit content, hate speech, threats, harassment, personal information about private people, or anything illegal will not be published.

7. How we publish it

We publish poems as written, including your spelling and line breaks, unless you ask us to change something. We may adjust formatting so it displays correctly on phones and computers, and we may add a title, date or short note you provided.

8. Taking it down

You can ask us to remove your poem at any time, for any reason. Contact us from the email address you submitted with and we'll take it down promptly. We may also remove any poem at any time.

9. Previously published

Some literary journals consider poems posted online to be previously published. If you plan to submit a poem to journals or contests, check their rules before sending it here.

10. Age

You must be at least 13 to submit. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should know you're submitting.

11. Your information

Your email is used only to contact you about your poem. It is never published or sold. See our privacy policy.

12. Responsibility

You're responsible for what you submit. If a poem you submit infringes someone else's rights, you agree that you, not the site, are responsible for that claim.

13. Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Questions? Contact us.

This page is a plain-English summary of how the site works and is not legal advice.
