Privacy policy

What we collect, how ads and cookies work here, and how to reach us about your information.

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information apoemaboutlove.com collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Things you send us. When you submit a poem or use the contact form, we receive what you type: your name, email, poem and any note. This goes to our private lead system and is used only to respond to you and, if you submitted a poem, to publish it.
  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site: pages visited, approximate location, device type. This uses cookies.
  • The love poem generator runs entirely in your browser. What you type there is not sent to us or stored.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message where required.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never publish your email address.
  • We never add you to a marketing list from a poem submission.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete information you've sent us, or to take down a published poem, at any time through the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
