About A Poem About Love

An open home for love poetry, started from a stack of handwritten notebooks.

A Poem About Love is an open home for love poetry. You can read poems about every kind of love, write one with our free generator, learn how to get published, and send in a poem of your own to be published free.

How it started

The site was started by Zachary Tye Wennstedt, a poet and painter from Colorado who has filled notebooks with poems since the late 1990s. His handwritten poems are the foundation of the site. You can read them in the founding collection and on The Notebook, typed exactly as they sit on the page. The paintings in the background of the site are his too.

The idea was simple. The news is loud with the worst of us, and love poems are the quiet proof of the best of us. Most of them never leave a drawer, and this site exists to give them somewhere to go.

What you'll find here

  • 50 love poems for every kind of love, written with AI and chosen by our editors and clearly labeled
  • The Notebook, real handwritten poems from 2001 to 2010
  • A free love poem generator
  • A step-by-step guide to becoming a published poet
  • The blog, with practical help for writing, choosing and reading love poems
  • Reader poems, sent through our submission page

How we handle content

Poems by people are never edited for spelling or style unless the poet asks. Anything written with AI is labeled as such. Every page is family-friendly, and we correct mistakes quickly. If you spot one, tell us.

A Poem About Love is published by Eye To Ad Media, headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
