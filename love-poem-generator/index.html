How to turn a generated poem into a real one

Quick answer

Generate a version you like, then change one line to something only the two of you would understand. One true detail turns a nice poem into their poem.

  1. Give it real details. “Hums while cooking” beats “is wonderful.” The smaller and stranger the habit, the better the poem.
  2. Press “Another version” a few times. Each press shuffles the lines. Stop when one makes you smile.
  3. Edit one line yourself. Swap in an inside joke, a date, a nickname.
  4. Read it out loud once. If a line trips your tongue, cut it.
  5. Deliver it by hand if you can. A printed card in a coat pocket lands harder than a text.
